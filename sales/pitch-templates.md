# 💬 WhatsApp Pitch Templates — Copy/Paste Ready

## ⚡ Golden Rules

1. **Personalize karo** — har message mein business ka naam likho
2. **Short rakho** — 4-6 lines max, scroll nahi karna chahiye
3. **Demo link zaroor bhejo** — proof of work
4. **Pushy mat ho** — ek baar pitch, 2 din baad follow-up, bas
5. **Voice note option** — Assam/NE mein voice note > text reply rate 2x hota hai

## 💪 OBJECTION HANDLERS

| Client Bole | Tum Reply Karo |
|---|---|
| "Bahut mehnga hai" | "Sir, ek month mein 1 extra customer aa gaya toh website cost vasool. Plus 1 saal hosting included hai." |
| "Mujhe Insta se kaam mil raha hai" | "Bilkul sahi, Insta + Website dono ho toh 2x impact. Insta se aaye client website pe trust gain karte hain." |
| "Time nahi hai mere paas" | "Aapko sirf 30 min chahiye — photos aur basic info dene ke liye. Baaki sab main handle karunga." |
| "Mere bhai/dost banayega" | "Bilkul sir, no problem. Agar woh nahi bana paaye toh ye mera number, kabhi bhi message kar dena." |
| "Discount do" | "₹500 off de sakta hu agar aaj advance kar dein. Iske aage discount mushkil hai, kyunki hosting + domain ka apna cost hota hai." |
| "Ek baar baad mein dekhte hain" | "Bilkul sir. Ek kaam karein — main 2 weeks baad ek follow-up message bhej dunga, tab tak aap soch lo." |

## ⚠️ KYA NAHI KARNA HAI

- ❌ Same message copy-paste sabko (personalize karo)

- ❌ Bhejte hi 5 min baad "reply?" likhna (annoying)
- ❌ Discount khud se offer karna (client maange tab do)
- ❌ Price advance mein discuss karna (pehle interest, fir price)
