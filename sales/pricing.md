# 💰 Pricing Sheet — Tezpur/Rangapara Market

## 🎯 Tier-3 City Pricing Strategy

Tezpur tier-3 hai → Mumbai/Delhi waale ₹25K-50K rates yahan nahi chalega. Realistic rates jo close hote hain:

---

## 📦 Package Tiers

### 🥉 STARTER — ₹3,500
**Tagline:** "Online presence, basic but professional"

**Includes:**
- Single-page website (5 sections)
- Mobile responsive
- Contact form (WhatsApp button)
- Free domain for 1 year (.in or .com)
- Free hosting on GitHub Pages
- 5-7 days delivery
- 1 round of revisions

**Best for:** Small shops, solo photographers, tuition centers

---

### 🥈 STANDARD — ₹6,500 ⭐ (Most Popular)
**Tagline:** "Sab kuch jo chahiye, aur thoda extra"

**Includes:**
- Everything in Starter +
- Multi-section site (6-8 sections)
- Photo gallery (up to 30 images)
- Animations (Framer Motion)
- Service/product pages
- Google Maps embed
- WhatsApp + Call buttons (floating)
- Basic SEO setup
- 7-10 days delivery
- 2 rounds of revisions

**Best for:** Coaching institutes, photographers, designers, restaurants

---

### 🥇 PREMIUM — ₹12,000
**Tagline:** "Full digital presence, business grow karne ke liye"

**Includes:**
- Everything in Standard +
- 3D hero animation (React Three Fiber)
- Glassmorphism design
- Booking/inquiry system
- Testimonials carousel
- Blog/news section
- Advanced SEO (Google ranking ready)
- Google Analytics setup
- Custom domain (.com or .in)
- 1 year support (bug fixes)
- 10-14 days delivery
- 3 rounds of revisions

**Best for:** Hotels/homestays, interior designers, premium photographers, established coaching

---

### 💎 ENTERPRISE — ₹20,000+
**Tagline:** "Custom solution, jaisa chahiye"

**Includes:**
- Custom features (e-commerce, booking calendar, payment gateway)
- Multi-page site
- Admin panel (content update khud kar sakte hain)
- Logo design + branding
- 6 months support
- Priority delivery

**Best for:** Hotels with online booking, jewelry shops with catalog, multi-location businesses

## 📊 Price Negotiation Floor

Ye **minimum prices** hain — isse neeche mat jaana:

| Package | Floor Price |
|---|---|
| Starter | ₹3,000 |
| Standard | ₹5,000 |
| Premium | ₹9,000 |

Isse neeche kaam profit nahi degi (domain ₹999 + hosting + tumhari 10-15 hours).

---

## 🎯 What to Quote First?

**Default rule:** Pehle **STANDARD (₹6,500)** quote karo. 

- Agar client hesitate kare → Starter offer karo (₹3,500)
- Agar client serious aur paying → Premium upsell karo (₹12,000)

**Never start with the cheapest** — anchoring high karo, fir negotiate.
